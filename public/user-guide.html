Core HR — Web Portal

Operations Dashboard

The command centre: live headcount, today's attendance rate, pending approvals, and estimated payroll liability. A green banner greets the admin with date and timezone. Quick-action widgets let you approve leaves without leaving the screen.

What You Can Do

  • 4 Live KPI Cards — Active Headcount, Daily Attendance %, Pending Tasks, Payroll Liability (PKR)
  • Multi-Branch Panel — each branch shows city, province, SESSI/PESSI linkage and staff count
  • Pending Leaves Queue — approve or reject directly from the dashboard card
  • Pakistan Compliance Banner — FBR, EOBI and provincial social security reminders

Required permission: view_dashboard. Assign it to all supervisors who need read-only oversight.

Read the Dashboard

1

Log in and the dashboard loads automatically. The green banner at the top shows the current date and Pakistan timezone (GMT+5).

2

The four white KPI cards show live figures. Pending Tasks turns amber when there are unactioned leaves or attendance regularizations.

3

The Branch panel shows SESSI or PESSI linkage per location. Hover any branch card to see department breakdown.

4

In the Pending Leaves card, click ✓ to approve or ✗ to reject without opening the Leave Management tab.

Core HR — Web Portal

Employee Directory

Full employee lifecycle management: onboard with CNIC, bank account, EOBI/PESSI number, zone, allowances, and profile photo. Search, filter by branch or status, and edit profiles at any time.

What You Can Do

  • Onboard Staff — full form: personal, payroll, bank, statutory (EOBI / SSNIT), allowances
  • Auto Employee Code — system generates BINISHAQ-DEPT-00001 format automatically
  • Status Management — Active, On Leave, Resigned, Terminated
  • Zone / UC-Town — assign employees to field zones and union council areas
  • Profile Photo Upload — base64 stored in Firestore, displayed everywhere
  • Reports sub-tab — attendance/leave grouped by UC, Zone, or Supervisor

Onboard a New Employee

1

Click + Onboard Staff in the top-right header. A full-page modal opens with tabs for Personal, Payroll, Bank, and Statutory details.

2

Fill in Full Name, CNIC (format: 42101-1234567-3), email, branch, department, and designation. Toggle Auto-generate Code to let the system create the BINISHAQ-DEPT-XXXXX code.

3

Enter the bank name, account number, and IBAN. Selecting the bank auto-fills the SWIFT prefix. Set EOBI number and Social Security number in the Statutory tab.

4

Upload a profile photo. Click Save Employee. The employee is immediately active in attendance, payroll, and the kiosk.

Core HR — Web Portal

Attendance Logs

View, filter, and manually correct punch-in/out records. Filter by day, week, month, or year. Employees can request regularization for missed punches; HR approves or rejects from this screen.

What You Can Do

  • Stream View — chronological log of all punches: In/Out time, method, status
  • Period Filter — Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly; pick any date range
  • Manual Entry — add a missed attendance record for any employee
  • Regularization Approval — amber badge on sidebar shows pending count; approve/reject here
  • Status Colours — Present (green), Late (amber), Absent (red)
  • Reports sub-tab — grouped summaries by UC, Zone, or Supervisor

Add a Manual Attendance Entry

1

Go to Attendance Logs. Set the period to Daily and pick the date. Click + Add.

2

Select the employee, date, punch-in and punch-out times, and set Method to Manual. Click Save.

3

To approve a regularization, click Approve next to the amber highlighted row. The record status updates immediately.

Core HR — Web Portal

Leave Management

Manage all leave requests: Casual, Sick, Annual, and Unpaid. HR approves or rejects in one click. Leave deductions flow automatically into the monthly payroll run.

What You Can Do

  • Apply Leave — HR can apply on behalf of any employee
  • Approve / Reject — one click per request; employee notified instantly
  • Leave Types — Casual (15 days), Sick (10 days), Annual (14 days), Unpaid
  • Status Filters — All / Pending / Approved / Rejected
  • Calendar sub-tab — see who is off on any given day
  • Reports — leave utilisation by employee, department, or period

Approve a Leave Request

1

Click Leave Management in the sidebar. The amber badge shows the count of pending requests.

2

Filter to Pending to see only unreviewed requests. Read the dates, type, and reason shown on each card.

3

Click Approve (green) or Reject (red). The status pill updates immediately and the employee receives a notification.

4

To apply leave on behalf of an employee, click + Apply, select the employee, leave type, date range, and reason, then save.

Core HR — Web Portal

Payroll Processing

Run monthly payroll with one click. The system calculates FBR income tax (8-slab), EOBI (1%), provincial social security (6%), loan installments, and late-day deductions. Approve, then download the bank advice CSV.

What You Can Do

  • New Payroll Run — select month/year; all active employees included automatically
  • Preview Grid — see each employee's Gross, FBR Tax, EOBI, PESSI, Loan Deduction, Net
  • Individual Payslip — click 📄 to open full payslip modal with all line items
  • Approve Run — locks figures; triggers automatic loan installment deductions
  • Bank Advice CSV — one file for bulk salary upload to your bank's portal
  • Mark Disbursed — confirms salary has been transferred to employees

Run the Monthly Payroll

1

Click Payroll Processing in the sidebar. Click + New Payroll Run. Select the month and year. All Active and On Leave employees are included.

2

Review the grid. Every employee shows Gross, FBR Tax, EOBI (PKR 370), PESSI/SESSI (6 %), any active loan installment, and Net Salary. Click 📄 to see the individual payslip breakdown.

3

Click Approve Run. This locks the figures. Outstanding loan installments are automatically deducted. No changes are possible after approval.

4

After transferring salaries via your bank, click Mark Disbursed. Download Bank Advice ↓ CSV before disbursing for bulk upload to HBL/MCB/UBL portal.

Core HR — Compliance

FBR / Tax Settings

Configure Pakistan's 8-slab federal income tax, EOBI contribution rates, and provincial social security (PESSI/SESSI). Changes apply on the next payroll run automatically.

What You Can Do

  • 8-Slab FBR Table — edit each slab's floor, ceiling, fixed tax and rate %
  • EOBI Rates — employee 1 % / employer 5 % of minimum wage (PKR 37,000)
  • PESSI / SESSI — 6 % of gross, auto-applied by employee province
  • Per-Employee Override — mark an employee tax-exempt from their profile

Only Super Admin can edit tax slabs. Already-approved payroll runs are never retroactively changed.

Update FBR Tax Slabs

1

Go to Settings → FBR / Tax. The Income Tax tab is active. Click any slab row to edit its fields.

2

Adjust Income From, To, Fixed Tax, and Rate % per the latest Finance Act notification from the FBR website.

3

Switch to EOBI tab to confirm the contribution cap. Switch to PESSI/SESSI to set which provinces use which scheme.

4

Click Save Tax Slabs. New rates apply on the next payroll run only.

Administration

Access Control (RBAC)

Invite HR users, assign built-in roles (Super Admin, HR Manager, Payroll Officer, Supervisor), and fine-tune individual permission toggles per user. No code changes needed.

Built-in Roles

  • Super Admin — full access including tax settings and user management
  • HR Manager — manage employees, leaves, payroll; no tax edits
  • Payroll Officer — run and approve payroll; read-only elsewhere
  • Supervisor — view team attendance, approve/reject team leaves
  • Custom Roles — create a role with any combination of permission flags

Invite a New HR User

1

Go to Settings → Access Control. Click + Invite User. Enter the user's email and select a base role.

2

Fine-tune individual permission toggles in the right panel. Turn off anything that person doesn't need.

3

Click Save Permissions. An invitation email is sent. The user sets their password on first login.

Administration

Notifications

Automated in-app alerts keep the right people informed: leave decisions, payroll disbursement, loan approval, contract expiry, and more. The bell icon in the sidebar shows the unread count.

Notification Events

  • Leave Approved / Rejected — instant alert to the requesting employee
  • Payroll Run Approved — alert to Payroll Officers and HR Manager
  • Loan Disbursed — confirmation to employee and Finance
  • Probation / Contract Expiry — 30-day advance warning to HR Manager
  • Asset Due Return — 7-day advance alert to HR and employee

Manage Notifications

1

Click Notifications in the sidebar. Unread items show a blue left border. The red badge on the sidebar shows the total unread count.

2

Click any notification to open the related record (e.g., clicking a leave notification opens that leave request).

3

Click Mark all read to clear all badges at once.

Administration

Data Backup & Firestore Maintenance

Export a full JSON snapshot of all HR data, or trigger a Firestore-level backup to Google Cloud Storage. Restore from any prior snapshot if needed.

What You Can Do

  • Export JSON — download all employees, payroll, attendance, leaves as one file
  • Scheduled Auto-Backup — daily midnight export to Google Cloud Storage
  • Restore from File — import a previous snapshot back into Firestore
  • Purge Old Records — delete attendance/payroll records older than a set date

Restore overwrites live data. Export a fresh backup immediately before restoring an older snapshot.

Export a Manual Backup

1

Go to Settings → Data Backup. Click ⬇ Export JSON Now. A file downloads immediately — save it to a secure location.

2

To enable nightly auto-backup, paste your Google Cloud Storage bucket name in the Auto-Backup Settings field and click Enable Auto-Backup.

3

To restore, click ⬆ Restore…, select your JSON file, confirm the warning dialog. Live data is overwritten immediately.

HR Tools

Biometric Enrollment

Enroll employee face descriptors and optional fingerprints (URU4500 USB device) used by the Kiosk Terminal. The sidebar shows a "Face + Fingerprint" badge on this menu item.

What You Can Do

  • Face Enrollment — capture 5 samples; oval guide turns green when face is in position
  • Fingerprint Enrollment — enroll up to 2 fingers via URU4500 USB sensor
  • Re-Enroll — overwrite existing samples if recognition fails at the kiosk
  • Bulk Status View — column grid shows ✓/✗ for face and fingerprint per employee

Best results: Frontal lighting, remove glasses, look directly into camera. Poor lighting is the #1 cause of kiosk recognition failure.

Enroll an Employee's Face

1

Go to HR Tools → Biometric Enrollment. Select the employee from the list. Click Enroll Face.

2

Ask the employee to stand ~50 cm from the camera without glasses. The oval guide turns green when the face is centred and well-lit.

3

Click Capture for each of 5 samples. The system rejects poor-quality frames. Re-take any failed sample.

4

After 5 accepted samples, click Save Enrollment. The employee is immediately active on the Kiosk Terminal.

Record and schedule salary increments, promotions, and allowance changes. The new salary applies automatically from the effective month — no manual payroll adjustment needed.

What You Can Do

  • % Increment — percentage raise applied to current gross
  • Fixed Amount — add a fixed PKR amount to base salary
  • Full Replacement — replace salary completely with a new figure

Apply a Salary Increment

1

Go to HR Tools → Salary Revisions. Click New Revision. Select the employee and revision type.

2

Enter the increment percentage or fixed amount. Set the Effective From month. The system shows the new gross salary preview below the form.

3

Performance Reviews

What You Can Do

  • Review Cycles — create annual or mid-year periods with open/close dates
  • KPI Templates — Attendance, Quality, Team Collaboration, Initiative, Goal Achievement

HR Tools

Gratuity & Full & Final Settlement

Calculate gratuity and generate the F&F Settlement statement at resignation or termination. Outstanding loans, unreturned assets, and leave encashment are all consolidated into one clearance document.

What You Can Do

  • Gratuity — last drawn basic × years of service (Pakistan labour law)
  • Leave Encashment — unused annual leave paid at basic-per-day rate
  • Loan Recovery — outstanding loan deducted from final settlement
  • Asset Clearance — F&F blocked until all assets are returned
  • F&F Statement PDF — printable settlement for employee signature

Process F&F Settlement

1

Change the employee status to Resigned in Employee Directory. Set their last working day.

2

Go to HR Tools → Gratuity & Settlement. Select the employee. Gratuity, leave encashment, and outstanding loan are calculated automatically.

3

Resolve any asset clearance warnings. Return assets in Asset Management, then refresh the F&F screen.

4

Click Download PDF for the signed copy, then Approve F&F to lock and record the settlement.

Platform — Windows Desktop

Windows Desktop Client

The Electron-based desktop app runs on Windows 10/11. It delivers the full Web Admin Portal in a native window, with offline caching, auto-update, and a one-click switch into Kiosk Mode.

Key Features

  • Offline Cache — employee list and recent attendance cached; works during internet outages
  • Auto-Update — new versions download in background and prompt for restart
  • Kiosk Mode — full-screen attendance terminal locked to one screen
  • URU4500 Fingerprint — USB sensor auto-detected via native Electron bridge
  • System Tray — app minimises to tray; Windows notifications for alerts

Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, 4 GB RAM, internet for first login. Subsequent sessions work offline for up to 7 days with cached credentials.

First-Time Setup

1

Run BinIshaHRSuite-Setup.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More Info → Run Anyway. The app installs and launches.

2

Sign in with your HR admin email and password. Internet is required for first login (Firebase authentication).

3

To switch to Kiosk Mode, click the 🖥 Switch to Kiosk button at the bottom of the sidebar. The window goes full-screen with the attendance terminal.

4

To exit Kiosk Mode, press Ctrl+Alt+Q and enter the admin PIN.

Platform — Kiosk Terminal

Kiosk Attendance Terminal

Full-screen touch-friendly attendance terminal installed at office entrances. Employees clock in using face recognition or URU4500 fingerprint. The dark UI shows a large clock and camera oval guide.

How Clocking Works

  • Face Recognition — face matched against 5-sample enrollment via RMSE ≤ 0.18
  • Fingerprint (URU4500) — touch USB sensor for 1:N match across all enrolled employees
  • Auto In/Out — first clock of day = In; next = Out; late arrivals flagged automatically
  • Manual Override — Admin PIN unlocks manual entry for biometric failures
  • Offline Queue — events queue locally during internet outage, auto-sync on reconnect

The kiosk camera must be at eye level, free of backlight. Re-enroll in the Biometric module if recognition fails repeatedly at the kiosk.

Using the Kiosk to Clock In

1

Walk up to the kiosk. The camera activates automatically. Stand so your full face is centred inside the green oval guide on the screen.

2

Hold still and look directly at the camera lens. Face matching takes approximately 1–2 seconds.

3

A green banner shows your name and Clocked In (or Clocked Out for subsequent scans). The event syncs to Firestore immediately.

4

If recognition fails three times, touch Manual Entry and ask an admin to enter the PIN for a manual attendance record.

Fingerprint alternative: If a URU4500 USB device is connected, employees can touch the sensor instead of the camera. Both produce identical attendance records in Firestore.
