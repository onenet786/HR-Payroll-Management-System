Executive System Overview

The OneNet HR & Payroll Management System is engineered to eliminate manual spreadsheets and fragmented data silos across Pakistan enterprise operations. By interconnecting employee onboarding, multi-channel biometric attendance, statutory tax compliance, loan recoveries, and automated banking disbursements into a single immutable audit trail, the software ensures 100% compliance with Pakistani labor laws and tax ordinances.

Core Operating Principle Every screen in the system serves a specific upstream or downstream data contract. The Operations Dashboard (Screen 12) reads from all transactional screens and writes nothing; Attendance exceptions in "Needs Review" strictly block the payroll cycle from locking until cleared; and Statutory Settings define mathematical baselines applied instantly across the entire roster.

The entire operation of the suite is governed by two foundational engineering diagrams: the Screen Data Interconnection Flow (Diagram 1) and the Attendance-to-Payroll Pipeline (Diagram 2).

This master diagram illustrates how statutory configurations, organizational hierarchies, employee demographics, time records, and monetary inputs seamlessly converge into the monthly payroll engine and produce legal reporting outputs.

Key Flow Connections in Diagram 1:

  • A. Set Up Once → B. People: Statutory Config (1) validates employee salaries against the Provincial Minimum Wage (PKR 37,000), while Company & Master Data (2) supplies branches, departments, grades, and shifts for employee onboarding.
  • A. Set Up Once → D. Money & 10. Payroll: Feeds the 30-day gratuity formula into Gratuity (11) and tax slabs, EOBI/PESSI ceilings, and 2x overtime multipliers into Payroll (10).
  • B. People → C. Time: Employee Directory (3) supplies biometric enrollment templates to Attendance Sources (6), shift rosters to Attendance (7), and hire dates to Leave Management (5).
  • C. Time → 10. Payroll: The locked Attendance Register (7) feeds payable workdays, unexcused absents, overtime hours, and late penalties directly into Payroll Step 2. Approved unpaid leaves from Leave Management (5) prorate salary deductions.
  • D. Money Inputs → 10. Payroll: Active loan monthly repayment installments (8) and approved salary increments (9) automatically deduct from or augment gross-to-net calculations.
  • 10. Payroll → Outputs (E) & Dashboard (12): Generates digital/PDF payslips, bank advice files, EOBI/PESSI returns, FBR withholding reports, and accounting journals, while updating the real-time Operations Dashboard.

Diagram 2: How a Punch Becomes a Paid Day

This diagram establishes the rigorous state machine that transforms physical biometric readings, camera frames, and mobile GPS check-ins into verified paid attendance records.

Figure 1.2: BIN ISHAQ HR & Payroll | Diagram 2: How A Punch Becomes A Paid Day

The Step-by-Step Punch Lifecycle:

  1. Biometric Setup & Device Registry: Every employee is enrolled with normalized face descriptor vectors, fingerprint templates, and dual English/Urdu legal consent. Physical devices and cameras are registered with specific branch locations and an 85% match confidence threshold (Euclidean distance < 0.255).
  2. Multi-Channel Ingestion:
    • Mobile App / Web: Checks GPS against the branch geofence radius. If outside radius or no GPS, the punch is diverted to Needs Review for managerial decision.
    • Fingerprint Reader: Validates against template through local USB/LAN bridge.
    • IP Camera (RTSP): Runs real-time face matching. If ≥ 85%, accepts punch. If low match, keeps frame for 30 days and alerts Needs Review.
    • Webcam Kiosk: Performs face recognition with active yaw liveness attestation, fallback to fingerprint or PIN.
    • Manual Entry: Tagged 'Manual'; requires line manager approval with reason and evidence within 3 working days.
  3. Anti-Passback Cooldown (2 Minutes): If the same employee triggers any reader within 120 seconds of their previous punch, the duplicate is ignored with a countdown cooldown warning, preventing accidental double punches.
  4. Daily Classification Rules:
    • 15-Minute Grace Period: Shift starts at 09:00; punches up to 09:15 are marked on-time Present. Punches after 09:15 are marked Late.
    • Half Day: Triggered when total work duration is between 4 and 6 hours.
    • Absent: Recorded when no punch exists and no approved leave is on file.
    • Overtime: Automatically accumulated for approved time worked beyond 8 standard hours (480 mins).
  5. The "Lock the Month" Integrity Check: The system enforces that the monthly attendance register cannot be locked while any punch remains pending in Needs Review. Step 2 of the Payroll Engine only ingests a certified, locked register.

2. Complete Menu-by-Menu User Guide

Menu 1: Operations Dashboard Step 12 of 12 • Observation Layer

Business Purpose: The primary command center providing real-time visibility across the enterprise workforce. It aggregates active headcount, daily punctuality, pending workflow queues, and financial payroll liability without modifying transactional data.

Key Metrics Displayed:

  • Total Active Staff: Headcount currently on the payroll roster.
  • Today's Attendance Gauge: Real-time count of staff clocked In, Late arrivals, Approved Leaves, and Unexcused Absents.
  • Action Items Requiring Decision: Number of pending leave applications, manual attendance regularizations, and loan requests.
  • Estimated Monthly Payroll Liability: Projected Gross Pay, Total Statutory Deductions, and Net Bank Disbursement.
Operator Rule The dashboard never computes arbitrary numbers of its own. If any number appears incorrect, the adjustment must be made in the corresponding operational screen (e.g., Attendance Register, Leave Management, or Employee Directory).
Menu 2: Company Setup & Master Data Step 2 of 12 • Foundation

Business Purpose: Defines the legal identity of the enterprise and establishes standardized hierarchy lists used across employee onboarding, attendance geofencing, and cost-center payroll registers.

Operational Walkthrough:

  1. Company Identity: Enter Legal Entity Name (e.g., OneNet Solutions Pk.), NTN (National Tax Number), STRN (Sales Tax Registration), and EOBI/SESSI corporate registration IDs.
  2. Branches & Geofencing: Configure operational locations (e.g., Karachi Head Office, Lahore Hub). Specify Branch Province (Sindh, Punjab, KPK, Balochistan), GPS Latitude/Longitude coordinates, and geofence radius (meters) for mobile punch authorization.
  3. Departments & Designations: Build corporate organizational charts. Designations can be assigned seniority grades (e.g., A1, M2) for compensation benchmarking.
  4. Shift Schedules: Define standard work hours, daily grace period (default 15 mins), and night-shift flags that cross midnight.
  5. Wage Types: Configure compensation classifications: Monthly Salaried vs. Daily Wager.
Menu 3: Statutory Settings & FBR Tax Slabs Step 1 of 12 • Statutory Foundation

Business Purpose: Houses the statutory tax matrices and social insurance parameters governing all monthly gross-to-net payroll deductions across Pakistan.

Official FBR Income Tax Slabs (Finance Act 2024 / 2025):

Enacted under the First Schedule, Part I, Division I, Clause (2) of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 for salaried individuals:

Slab # Annual Taxable Income Range (PKR) Fixed Base Tax Tax Rate on Excess Formula Description
Slab 1 Rs. 0 – 600,000 Rs. 0 0.0% Where taxable income does not exceed Rs. 600,000: 0%
Slab 2 Rs. 600,001 – 1,200,000 Rs. 0 5.0% 5% of the amount exceeding Rs. 600,000
Slab 3 Rs. 1,200,001 – 2,200,000 Rs. 30,000 15.0% Rs. 30,000 + 15% of the amount exceeding Rs. 1,200,000
Slab 4 Rs. 2,200,001 – 3,200,000 Rs. 180,000 25.0% Rs. 180,000 + 25% of the amount exceeding Rs. 2,200,000
Slab 5 Rs. 3,200,001 – 4,100,000 Rs. 430,000 30.0% Rs. 430,000 + 30% of the amount exceeding Rs. 3,200,000
Slab 6 Exceeding Rs. 4,100,000 Rs. 700,000 35.0% Rs. 700,000 + 35% of the amount exceeding Rs. 4,100,000
Section 4AB Surcharge (Finance Act 2024 / 2025) A mandatory 10% super-tax surcharge is automatically levied on the total income tax payable for high-earning salaried individuals whose annual taxable income exceeds PKR 10,000,000 (10 Million PKR).

Social Security & Minimum Wage Parameters:

  • Government Minimum Wage: PKR 37,000 per month (used as baseline for EOBI contributions and statutory wage check).
  • EOBI Contributions: Employee pays 1% of minimum wage (PKR 370/mo); Employer pays 5% (PKR 1,850/mo).
  • Provincial Social Security (SESSI / PESSI): Employer contributes 6% of basic earnings up to the configured statutory wage ceiling (PKR 40,000).
  • Statutory Overtime: Standard 208 working hours/month; Overtime hours compensated at 2.0x normal basic hourly rate.

Built-In UI Controls:

  • "Download CSV": Generates an official spreadsheet export of the active FBR tax table.
  • "Update from FBR": One-click button to synchronize and restore the official Finance Act rates directly into the PostgreSQL database.
Menu 4: Employee Directory & Onboarding Step 3 of 12 • Personnel Roster

Business Purpose: Manages master employee personnel files, statutory tax exemptions, CNIC identity records, salary structures, and Pakistani banking details.

Step-by-Step Onboarding Instructions:

  1. Select "Add Employee" to launch the onboarding modal.
  2. Identity: Input Full Name, Employee Code, CNIC (formatted as 42101-1234567-1), Mobile Number, and Personal Email.
  3. Hierarchy Assignment: Select Company Branch, Department, Designation, and Shift. The branch selection determines the employee's provincial social security jurisdiction (e.g., SESSI in Sindh, PESSI in Punjab).
  4. Compensation Structure: Input Basic Contract Salary. The system automatically structures standard allowances:
    • Base Basic Wage: 50%
    • House Rent Allowance: 30%
    • Conveyance Allowance: 10%
    • Medical Allowance: 10%
    • Or input custom negotiated allowances directly.
  5. Statutory Opt-Ins: Check eligibility for FBR Income Tax withholding, EOBI, Provincial Social Security, and Provident Fund (with employee contribution %).
  6. Banking Advice: Select Pakistani Bank (HBL, MCB, UBL, Alfalah, Meezan, etc.), Bank Account Number, and 24-character Pakistani IBAN (PK...).
  7. Save the record. The employee is now active and immediately eligible for attendance and payroll inclusion.
Menu 5: Holiday Calendar Step 4 of 12 • Time Foundation

Business Purpose: Tracks federal gazetted public holidays and corporate foundation days. When payroll runs, paid non-working days are automatically credited.

Key Operational Rules:

  • Non-Deduction from Leaves: If an approved leave request spans across a gazetted holiday (e.g., Pakistan Day on March 23), the holiday is **not deducted** from the employee's annual or casual leave balance.
  • Paid Attendance Credit: Employees scheduled to work on a gazetted holiday are marked with status Holiday and receive full pay without requiring manual time punches.
Menu 6: Leave Management & Approvals Step 5 of 12 • Absence Governance

Business Purpose: Manages employee leave applications, supervisor approvals, balance tracking, and encashment reconciliations.

Leave Categories Supported:

Leave Type Annual Entitlement Pay Status Payroll Deduction Impact
Casual Leave 10 Days 100% Paid No salary deduction; maintains full monthly gross.
Sick / Medical Leave 8 Days 100% Paid No salary deduction upon supervisor medical approval.
Annual / Earned Leave 14 Days 100% Paid Eligible for encashment upon resignation or year-end.
Unpaid Leave (LWP) As approved Unpaid Deducts calendar-day salary: (Basic Salary / Total Days in Month) × Unpaid Days

Supervisor Approval Workflow:

  1. Employee submits leave via mobile app or web portal specifying type, dates, and reason.
  2. HR or Line Manager receives alert under "Pending Leave Requests".
  3. Manager reviews quota balance and selects Approve or Reject.
  4. Approved leave immediately reflects on the Attendance Stream as On Leave, protecting the employee from unexcused absence penalties.
Menu 7: Attendance Register & Needs Review Step 7 of 12 • Time & Attendance

Business Purpose: Central ingestion clearinghouse for all digital attendance records, shift grace evaluations, overtime tracking, and exception regularizations.

Daily Evaluation Rules:

  • 15-Minute Grace Window: Shift starts at 09:00 AM. Punch-ins between 09:00:01 and 09:15:00 are marked Present. Punch-ins at 09:15:01 or later are classified as Late.
  • Half-Day Calculation: If total hours worked between Punch-In and Punch-Out are under 4 hours, status updates to Half Day.
  • Overtime Engine: Working hours exceeding 8 hours (480 mins) are stored in overtimeMinutes and converted to hours for 2x overtime payroll compensation.

The "Needs Review" Queue:

Punches with missing checkout, off-site mobile GPS punches, or employee manual regularization claims appear in the Needs Review panel. HR managers can inspect evidence and select Approve or Decline.

Payroll Blocker The system will refuse to lock or create a payroll run if any attendance record remains unreviewed for that period. All pending exceptions must be cleared first.
Menu 8: Biometrics, Cameras & Kiosk Terminal Step 6 of 12 • Physical Ingestion

Business Purpose: Connects physical reception hardware, USB fingerprint readers, and IP cameras to the attendance stream.

Supported Ingestion Channels:

  • Desktop Kiosk Terminal: Runs full-screen on Windows at reception. Employs continuous camera stream with active face tracking, MediaPipe landmarks, and yaw-rotation liveness detection.
  • DigitalPersona URU 4500 USB Reader: Communicates via background C# bridge service (tools/uru4500-bridge) capturing ANSI-378 biometric minutiae templates.
  • IP RTSP Camera: Direct stream ingestion for entrance gates with automatic face recognition matching against enrolled employee vectors.
  • 2-Minute Anti-Passback Cooldown: Enforces a strict 120-second timer between consecutive punches. Prevents accidental double-swipes or buddy-punching duplicates.
Menu 9: Loans & Salary Advances Step 8 of 12 • Financial Inputs

Business Purpose: Disburses and manages corporate employee loans and emergency salary advances, with automated monthly payroll deductions.

Operating Procedure:

  1. Create loan record: specify Employee, Type (Company Loan or Salary Advance), Principal Amount, Monthly Installment, and Repayment Tenure (months).
  2. Select Approve. Once active, the system automatically deducts the monthly installment from the employee's net payslip during every payroll run.
  3. The system tracks total repaid amount and decrements remainingInstallments.
  4. If an employee leaves before full repayment, the remaining unpaid balance is automatically recovered from their final gratuity settlement.
Menu 10: Salary Revisions & Increments Step 9 of 12 • Compensation Adjustments

Business Purpose: Tracks annual performance increments, promotion adjustments, and grade band revisions before running payroll.

  • Records Old Basic Salary, New Basic Salary, Effective Date, and Appraisal Reference.
  • The payroll engine checks effective dates and applies revised salary structures automatically.
Menu 11: Payroll Processing Engine Step 10 of 12 • The Core Engine

Business Purpose: Executes the monthly payroll calculation, freezing attendance, applying progressive statutory tax withholding, recovering loan installments, and generating bank payment files.

The 7-Step Payroll Processing Cycle:

  1. Period Selection: Choose Payroll Month and Year. The system verifies that no duplicate cycle exists for the period.
  2. Attendance Verification: Ingests the locked attendance register. Verifies payable days, unpaid leaves, and overtime hours.
  3. Money Inputs Ingestion: Ingests approved salary revisions, active loan installments, and terminal settlements.
  4. Gross-to-Net Computation: Simulates gross earnings, FBR tax withholding, EOBI, PESSI, Provident Fund, and Net Pay for all eligible employees.
  5. Draft Creation & Dual Approval: Manager reviews the draft register and selects Approve. Approval freezes the calculations and commits loan deductions.
  6. Disbursement Authorization: Once bank funds are transferred, select Mark Disbursed to finalize the cycle.
  7. Output Delivery: Generate individual digital payslips, download bank advice CSVs, and export statutory schedules.

Mathematical Gross-to-Net Breakdown:

Component Calculation Formula Statutory / Legal Basis
Gross Salary Basic + House Rent + Conveyance + Medical + Other + Overtime Pay − Unpaid Leave Deduction Employment Contract
Overtime Pay (Basic / 208 hrs) × Overtime Hours × 2.0 Factories Act, 1934
FBR Income Tax Annualize Gross × 12 → Apply 6 Progressive Slabs (+ 10% Surcharge if > 10M) ÷ 12 ITO 2001, 1st Sched Part I Div I
EOBI Employee Minimum Wage (PKR 37,000) × 1% = PKR 370 / month EOBI Act, 1976
EOBI Employer Minimum Wage (PKR 37,000) × 5% = PKR 1,850 / month EOBI Act, 1976
Social Security (PESSI) min(Basic, PKR 40,000) × 6% = max PKR 2,400 / month Provincial Social Security Act
Net Pay Gross Salary − (Tax + EOBI + PF + Loan Installment) Bank Advice Payout
Menu 12: Gratuity & Final Settlement Step 11 of 12 • Terminal Settlement

Business Purpose: Computes legal end-of-service settlements for exiting personnel in accordance with Pakistani labor laws.

Statutory Gratuity Formula:

Under the Industrial and Commercial Employment (Standing Orders) Ordinance, 1968, employees with continuous service of 1 year or more are entitled to 30 days basic wages for every completed year of service:

Gratuity Calculation Formula Gratuity = (Completed Years of Service) × (Last Drawn Basic Salary) × (30 / 30)

Pro-rated for fractions of service exceeding 6 months.

Terminal Settlement Checklist:

  • (+) Statutory Gratuity: Calculated based on tenure and final basic wage.
  • (+) Unpaid Days: Pro-rated salary for workdays completed in the exit month.
  • (+) Leave Encashment: Cash payment for unutilized annual leave balances.
  • (+) Notice Period Pay: Paid if company waives contractual notice.
  • (−) Asset Clearances: Physical return of issued laptops, mobile phones, security keys. Exit is blocked until all assets are checked in.
  • (−) Loan Balance Recovery: Any remaining unpaid loan principal is deducted directly from the final check.
Supporting Operational Modules Administrative Services

Company Assets (Menu 13):

Tracks company property (serial numbers, models, custody). Directly connected to the Gratuity module—prevents settlement disbursement until all assets are marked 'Returned'.

Recruitment & Job Applications (Menu 14):

Post vacancies, screen applicants, schedule interviews, and seamlessly onboard hired candidates directly into Menu 4 (Employee Directory) with one click.

Performance Reviews & KPIs (Menu 15):

Conduct quarterly and annual appraisals. Scores feed directly into Menu 10 (Salary Revisions) to determine merit increment percentages.

User Access & Permissions (Menu 16):

Enforces dual-authorization segregation of duties. Configures roles: Super Admin, HR Manager, Payroll Officer, Auditor, and Kiosk Terminal.

3. Statutory Outputs, Banking Files & Accounting

The payroll engine automatically produces five standardized statutory and financial outputs upon approving a payroll run:

1. Bank Advice Payment File (HBL, MCB, UBL, Alfalah, Meezan)

A standardized CSV/TXT disbursement file formatted for direct portal upload to major Pakistani commercial banks. Contains Employee Name, Account Number, 24-digit IBAN, CNIC, and Net Salary.

2. Individual Digital & Printable Payslips

Employees receive real-time access on their mobile self-service app. HR administrators can view, print, or download PDF payslips showing complete breakdowns of basic wages, allowances, overtime, tax withholding, EOBI, PF, and loan deductions.

3. EOBI Monthly Contribution Return (Form PR-01 / PR-02)

Summary report detailing total insured headcount, employee 1% deductions (PKR 370/person), and employer 5% contributions (PKR 1,850/person) for filing on the EOBI portal.

4. Provincial Social Security Statement (SESSI / PESSI)

Provincial schedule itemizing covered employees earning up to PKR 40,000, calculating the 6% employer contribution for monthly challan submission.

5. FBR Annual Withholding Tax Certificate (Section 149)

Itemized statement of total taxable salary paid and total income tax deducted at source under the First Schedule of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001.

6. General Ledger Accounting Journal Entry

Provides dual-entry bookkeeping records for the enterprise accounting department:

4. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP Checklist)

Daily Morning Routine (HR & Reception) Daily SOP
  • 08:30 AM – Kiosk & Hardware Check: Ensure the Windows reception kiosk is launched, camera stream is active, and USB fingerprint bridge is online.
  • 09:30 AM – Punctuality Stream Review: Open Menu 7 (Attendance). Filter for today's logs to inspect on-time arrivals, grace period entries, and late arrivals.
  • 10:00 AM – Needs Review Queue: Check for any unrecognized camera matches, off-site mobile punches, or manual regularization requests. Approve or decline pending items.
Monthly Payroll Cutoff Routine (Payroll Specialists) Monthly SOP
  • 25th of Month – Cutoff Notice: Issue employee deadline for submitting pending leaves, expense claims, and regularization requests.
  • 28th of Month – Clear Needs Review: Review and resolve 100% of attendance exceptions in Menu 7. Verify that no items remain in "Needs Review".
  • 29th of Month – Generate Draft Payroll: Open Menu 11 (Payroll). Select active month/year and click Run New Payroll Cycle. Review gross-to-net simulation grid.
  • 30th of Month – Dual Authorization: Payroll Officer reviews calculations and selects Approve. HR Director conducts final audit and authorizes bank release.
  • 1st of New Month – Bank Disbursement: Download Bank Advice File from Menu 11. Upload to commercial bank portal. Once payments clear, select Mark Disbursed.
  • 10th of New Month – Statutory Filings: Submit EOBI challan via portal and deposit FBR withholding tax via e.fbr.gov.pk challan (CPR).
Employee Exit & Clearance Routine (HR Operations) Offboarding SOP
  • Receive formal resignation or termination notice. Update employee status to 'Resigned' / 'Notice Period'.
  • Open Menu 13 (Assets). Check in and inspect all issued equipment (laptops, phones, cards). Confirm zero open assets.
  • Open Menu 9 (Loans). Confirm remaining loan principal balance.
  • Open Menu 12 (Gratuity & Settlement). Compute 30 days/year gratuity, add unpaid days and leave encashment, deduct remaining loan balance.
  • Generate Final Settlement Certificate, obtain employee sign-off, and release disbursement.
