B5Logins, roles and kiosks — User Management

Here you create a login for each person, decide what each role may do, reset passwords, approve two-step-verification resets and manage attendance kiosks. Only people with the "Users & roles" permission can open this page.

Create a login for a person

  1. In ONBOARD SYSTEM USER fill USERNAME (for example ayesha.hr), EMAIL and PASSWORD (at least 12 characters).
  2. Choose the role in ASSIGN INITIAL ROLE.
  3. For an employee who will use the mobile app, choose their name in LINK EMPLOYEE (REQUIRED FOR MOBILE). Each employee can be linked to only one login. (Add the employee in 3. Employee Directory first.)
  4. Click Create Account. Give the person their email and password privately and ask them to set up two-step verification after their first sign-in.
Good to know

Passwords are stored in scrambled (encrypted) form. Nobody — not even the administrator — can read a person's password. If it is forgotten, set a new one as shown below.

Change a login or reset a password

  1. In the user list, click Edit next to the person.
  2. Change Username, Role, Account Status (Inactive stops the person signing in) or Linked Employee. The email cannot be changed.
  3. To reset a forgotten password: first confirm the person's identity (in person or by a call to their known number). Then type a new password of at least 12 characters in Reset password (optional). Leave it empty to keep the current password.
  4. Click Save User. After a password reset the person is signed out everywhere and must sign in with the new password.
Warning — Delete data

Delete data permanently removes the login and the linked employee's personal records (attendance, leave, loans, reviews, settlements, profile, fingerprints). Payslips are kept but names, CNIC and bank details are replaced by "[REDACTED]". Use it only when the law or the person requires their data to be erased. To stop someone signing in, set Account Status to Inactive instead.

Roles & permissions

  • Each row is a role; each column is a permission: Dashboard, Employees, Attendance, Mobile duty, Leaves, Payroll, Settings, Users & roles, Use kiosk. Under each role you see how many users have it.
  • Tick or untick a box to change what every user with that role may do. People see the change the next time their page loads.
  • Super Admin, Employee and Kiosk Terminal are fixed and cannot be changed.
  • You cannot remove "Users & roles" from your own role (you would lock yourself out), and "Use kiosk" cannot be given to a staff role.

Create a new role: in CREATE CUSTOM ROLE type a name (for example "Auditor") and description, tick the permissions and click Create Custom Role. You can adjust it later in the table.

Attendance kiosks

Each kiosk PC or tablet signs in with its own Kiosk Terminal account. This account can only record attendance — it cannot open any other data.

  1. Create a login with role Kiosk Terminal (for example username kiosk.gate1, email kiosk.gate1@yourcompany.com). Do not link an employee.
  2. On the kiosk PC, open Settings → Server Registration and sign in once with that account (section G3). For a tablet, sign in to the mobile app with it (section G7).
  3. The kiosk now appears under KIOSK TERMINALS with its last sync time.
  4. If a kiosk is lost, stolen or replaced, click Disconnect next to it. It stops syncing immediately and must be registered again. Switching the kiosk account to Inactive disconnects every kiosk using it.
Important

Never sign in to a kiosk with an administrator or HR account — the system refuses, because kiosks are shared machines.

Two-step verification (MFA) resets

Under MFA recovery requests:

  1. To raise a request for someone who lost their phone: choose them in Select user for recovery, type how you verified their identity (at least 10 characters) in Identity-verification reason and click Request reset.
  2. A second administrator reads the reason and clicks Approve or Reject. (The person who raised a request cannot decide it.)
  3. After approval, the user's two-step verification is switched off, they are signed out everywhere, and they sign in with their password only. Ask them to set up MFA again immediately.
Tip — verify identity outside the system

Approve only after checking identity through another channel: a video call, meeting in person, or calling the phone number on their employee record.
