PART H
Looking after your data
Backups and restoring. For administrators only.

H1Data Backup

Make a backup (do this at least weekly, and before any big change)

  1. Open Data Backup (under SYSTEM).
  2. Click Backup All Data. A file named database-backup-…-full.json is downloaded.
  3. Store it somewhere safe and private (for example an encrypted company drive), because it contains salaries and CNICs.

To back up only some lists, tick them in Collections (or use the Operational / All / None buttons) and click Backup Selected Collections.

Good to know

This screen backs up the main lists (employees, attendance, leave, loans, payroll runs and so on). Your IT person should also keep full server backups of the database, which include everything (payslips, approval history, audit log).

Restore from a backup

  1. Click Choose Backup File and pick the backup file.
  2. Tick the lists to restore. Tick Merge backup without deleting current documents first if you only want to add back missing items.
  3. Type the word RESTORE in the confirmation box and click Restore Backup.
Warning — Clear and Delete buttons

Clear Operational Data (type CLEANUP) and Delete Selected Collections (type DELETE) remove data permanently. A safety backup is taken first, but only use these with your IT person, for example when removing test data before going live. Payroll runs, payslips and approval records can never be deleted.

PART I
Help & answers
Quick answers to common questions and problems, and the meaning of words used in this guide.

I1Frequently asked questions

I changed a tax rate but payroll still uses the old one.

Rate changes are proposals until a second person approves them in 1. Statutory Config (section B1).

The Approve button is grey.

You prepared this item, so someone else must approve it. Move the mouse over the button to read why.

Payroll says the register must be locked, but I locked it.

Something changed after locking (new attendance, leave, a holiday). Click Reopen Register, give the reason, check, and lock again.

An employee is missing from payroll.

Only Active and On-Leave employees are included. Check their status, and that their joining date is before the month ends.

An employee's tax looks wrong.

Check the tax slabs (rates typed as percentages, e.g. 11 for 11%), the tax year and effective date, the employee's prior-employer income and tax (for mid-year joiners), and their perquisites. Use the payslip's tax panel (taxable income so far, projected yearly income) to follow the calculation.

A kiosk does not recognise someone.

Check the face box label (section G4). Most often the face needs enrolling again (section D2), the kiosk needs a Sync, or the light is poor.

Two people were recognised as the same person.

This was a problem with the old face method and is fixed by the October 2026 upgrade. Make sure every kiosk runs version 1.0.2 or later and that every face has been enrolled again.

An employee cannot see the Attend tab in the app.

Phone attendance needs a mobile-duty assignment for that day (section D3). On normal days they use the kiosk.

An employee cannot see their payslip in the app.

Payslips appear only after the month's payroll is marked Disbursed, and only if their login is linked to their employee record.

Someone forgot their password.

An administrator resets it in User Management → Edit → Reset password, after confirming their identity (section B5).

Someone lost the phone with their authenticator app.

Two administrators reset their MFA in User Management → MFA recovery requests (section B5). Then the person sets it up again.

How do I give a new employee a mobile login?

Add them in 3. Employee Directory, then in User Management create a login with role Employee and link it to their name (section B5).

Where do I change working hours, Ramadan or Friday timings?

On each employee's record (3. Employee Directory → Edit Record). Enter Ramadan dates every year.

Can I import employees from Excel?

Not at present; employees are added one by one with Onboard Employee.

I3Routines at a glance

Every day

  • Check the dashboard's Manager Approval Inbox and Compliance alerts.
  • Decide attendance corrections and leave requests.
  • Check that each kiosk's Online and Synced lights are green.

Every month

  • Follow the month-end checklist (section E1).
  • File FBR, EOBI and social-security returns; record the FBR CPR.
  • Make a backup (section H1).

Every year

  • Before July payroll: new tax year, tax slabs and rates — proposed and approved (section B1).
  • Add next year's public holidays, including Eid dates when announced (section B4).
  • Update Ramadan dates on employee records.
  • Issue section 149 salary tax certificates (section E4).

When people join or leave

  • Joiner: add the employee, enroll face/fingerprint, create a mobile login if needed, complete the onboarding checklist.
  • Leaver: Trigger Exit → second-person approval → collect assets → process and pay the settlement → switch their login to Inactive.
