PART D
Time & attendance
How attendance is recorded and checked, how faces and fingerprints are enrolled, how staff are allowed to punch from their phones, and how leave is handled. For HR and attendance staff.

D1How attendance becomes pay

1. Punch→2. Status worked out→3. Problems flagged→4. Leave & holidays added→5. Month locked→6. Payroll
  1. Punch. Employees punch at a kiosk (face or fingerprint), on their phone (face check and GPS, only when allowed), or HR types an entry by hand. A kiosk keeps punches even when the internet is down and sends them later (up to 8 days old).
  2. Status. The system compares the punch with the employee's shift:
    StatusRule
    PresentArrived within the grace minutes (default 15 minutes).
    LateArrived after the grace minutes but less than 2 hours late.
    Half DayArrived 2 hours or more late.
    AbsentNo punch on a working day, with no leave and no holiday.
    Overtime is time worked beyond 8 hours (after breaks). Friday and Ramadan timings are used when set.
  3. Problems are flagged for a person to check: weak face or fingerprint matches, phone punches outside the branch area, impossible travel, hand-typed entries and employee correction requests.
  4. Leave and holidays. Approved leave becomes paid (or unpaid) leave days; holidays and weekly days off are added from the calendar.
  5. Lock. At month end, payroll staff lock ("certify") the register in 10. Payroll Processing. It cannot be locked while any item is waiting for review.
  6. Pay. Payroll counts present, late, half and leave days from the locked register. Absent and unpaid days are deducted; overtime is paid.

D2Enrolling fingerprints and faces — 6. Biometric Devices

Before an employee can use a kiosk, their fingerprints and/or face must be enrolled here — usually on the HR office computer.

Connect the fingerprint reader (one time per computer)

  1. Install the reader software (DigitalPersona U.are.U SDK or SecuGen FDx SDK Pro) supplied by your IT person, and plug the reader (U.are.U 4500 or SecuGen Hamster Pro) into a USB port.
  2. Open 6. Biometric Devices and click Connect Bridge. The status changes from Disconnected to Native Bridge.
  3. On the TEST DEVICE tab, click Scan Fingerprint and place a finger to check the reader works.

If you see Bridge connected, but no fingerprint reader was detected, unplug and reconnect the reader and restart the bridge program. Simulate is only for practice without a reader.

Enroll fingerprints

  1. On ENROLL BIOMETRICS, choose the EMPLOYEE.
  2. Click Scan Sample 1/3 and ask the employee to press the finger flat on the reader. Repeat for samples 2 and 3 (same finger). A sample below 60% quality is refused — clean the reader and try again.
  3. Click Save to Database.

Enroll a face

  1. Choose the employee at the top of the tab.
  2. Make sure the room is evenly lit (no bright window behind the person) and only this employee is in front of the camera. Glasses normally worn can stay on.
  3. Click Start Camera and allow the camera if the browser asks. The face should fill the oval.
  4. Click Save 5 Faces. Follow the prompts: Center your face, Turn your head LEFT (or RIGHT), Return to center, then the other side. This "liveness" check proves a real person is present, not a photo.
  5. Keep still while 5 pictures are taken. You see 5 face samples saved for … Click Verify Face to test this enrollment.
  6. Click Verify Face. The card Recognized Employee should show the right person.
  7. Press Sync on each kiosk (or wait a few minutes) so it receives the new face.

The MARK ATTENDANCE tab on this page is for testing the reader only. Real attendance should be recorded on the Windows kiosk (Part G), which checks the fingerprint against the saved templates.

D3Checking attendance — 7. Attendance

The live list of punches

The Digital Attendance Stream shows every punch with the date, in and out times, method (Camera, Biometric, Mobile GPS…), location evidence for phone punches (with a map link and accuracy), overtime and status.

Review corrections and flagged punches

When anything needs a decision, an amber box Pending Attendance Regularization Requests appears. Each card shows the employee, date, reason, any evidence files (gate pass, client sign-off) and, for flagged biometric punches, the match strength.

  1. Read the reason and open the evidence.
  2. Click Approve and type what you checked (for example "gate log confirms 09:05 entry"), or click Decline and give a reason.
  • Who decides: the employee's line manager first. HR may decide only after 3 working days; after that the request shows OVERDUE — escalate to department head and the manager is reminded.
  • Approving re-checks the time against the shift — a late arrival stays Late. Declining a flagged punch marks the day Absent.
  • Nobody can decide their own request, and decisions cannot be changed afterwards.

Add an attendance entry by hand

  1. Click Add Attendance Log.
  2. Choose the employee, date, status, in and out times and method, and click Save Punch Log.
  3. The entry goes into the review box — another person must approve it before the month can be locked.

Allow an employee to punch from their phone (mobile duty)

Phone attendance is switched off for everyone by default. Allow it only for people working away from the office on given days.

  1. In Mobile Duty Authorizations, choose the employee and the duty type: Work from home, Out of station, Client visit, Market / field duty, Official travel, Direct reporting to worksite or Emergency duty.
  2. Set the from and to dates, optionally the client or location, and type the instructions or approval reason.
  3. Tick Allow multiple field visits if they will check in at several clients in a day.
  4. Click Assign Mobile Duty. The employee's app now shows the Attend tab on those dates. Click Cancel authorization to withdraw it.

Phone punches outside the branch area are still saved but sent to the manager for review.

Registers (reports)

Click DAILY / WEEKLY / MONTHLY / YEARLY REGISTERS. Choose daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, pick the date and (optionally) one employee, and see counts of Present, Late, Half Day, On Leave and Absent with each person's punches.

Good to know

The weekly and yearly registers currently show fixed example periods; use the daily and monthly registers for real checks.

Balances run January to December. Remaining = entitlement − approved − pending, so waiting requests already reduce the balance.

Approve or reject a request

  1. Employees apply in the mobile app. Requests appear in LEAVE APPLICATIONS LIST with the dates, days, reason and remaining balance (red if it is not enough).
  2. Click Approve or Reject. The employee is notified, and approved days are added to the attendance register automatically.

Record leave yourself

  1. Click Add Leave Record.
  2. Choose the employee, leave type, start and end dates and reason.
  3. Click Approve & Log Leave. It is saved as approved immediately.
Important
  • Leave days are counted as calendar days from start to end, including weekends and holidays.
  • If the balance is not enough, approval is blocked with Approval blocked: … Apply the excess as Unpaid leave. Record the extra days as Unpaid leave.

The DAILY / WEEKLY / MONTHLY / YEARLY REPORTS tab shows who is on approved leave on a date, and monthly and yearly summaries.
