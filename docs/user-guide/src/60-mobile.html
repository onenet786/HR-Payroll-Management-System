PART F
The employee mobile app
For employees. How to install the app, sign in, mark attendance when working away from the office, ask for leave and see your payslips. Managers can photocopy this part for staff.

F1Installing and signing in

  1. Your HR department gives you the app file (Bin Ishaq HR Suite). Open it on your Android phone and tap Install. If the phone warns about installing from this source, allow it for this file only (Settings → "Install unknown apps").
  2. When asked, allow Camera and Location — they are needed for attendance.
  3. Open the app. Type the email and password HR gave you and tap Sign In to System.
  4. Tick Remember me for 5 days on your own phone so you do not have to sign in every time.
Important

Your login must be linked to your employee record. If you see Employee account is not linked, ask HR to link it in User Management.

At the bottom are four tabs: Home, Attend (only on days you are allowed to punch from your phone), Leave and Payslip. Logout is at the top right.

F2The Home screen

  • Today's Status: Not Started, Day Active or Day Finished.
  • Geofence: Verified in range (your punch was inside the branch area), Manager review (outside the area, your manager will check), or Verified when punching (nothing checked yet today).
  • Leave Used and Leave Pending this year.
  • If you are allowed to work outside today, a card shows your Assigned Mobile Duty, dates, location and instructions, with a green Mark Attendance button.

F3Marking attendance from your phone

You can punch from your phone only on days your manager or HR has assigned you mobile duty (for example a client visit or work from home). On normal office days, use the kiosk at the entrance.

  1. Tap Attend (or Mark Attendance on Home). The front camera starts.
  2. Choose what you are doing:
    • Start Day and Finish Day — once each per day.
    • Check In and Check Out — for each client or site you visit (if your duty allows visits). For Check In, type the client or location name.
  3. Type the reason or details in the box marked * (for example "Started HR audit at client office").
  4. Hold the phone at face height with your whole face inside the oval, in good light. Tap Verify Face & … (or simply hold still — it starts by itself).
  5. Follow the instructions: Turn your head LEFT / RIGHT, Return to center. Move only your head, not the phone.
  6. The phone then takes your GPS location (it must be accurate to within 100 metres — stand near a window or outdoors if needed).
  7. You see Attendance Recorded! with the time, method and location. Tap Record Next Action or Back to Home.

Forgot to punch? Ask for a correction

  1. Scroll to Missed Punch? Regularize at the bottom of the Attend tab.
  2. Choose the date and describe what happened (at least 10 characters).
  3. Optionally attach proof — a gate pass, client sign-off or ticket (photo or PDF, up to 10 MB).
  4. Tap File Regularization. Your line manager reviews it.

If you do not have the Attend tab, ask your manager or HR to raise the correction for you.

F4Asking for leave

  1. Tap Leave. My leave summary shows days left for Casual, Sick and Annual leave.
  2. Choose the LEAVE TYPE: Casual, Sick / Medical, Annual or Unpaid.
  3. Choose the FROM and TO dates and type the reason.
  4. Tap Submit Leave Request. HR or your manager will approve or reject it, and you will be notified.

If you do not have enough days left, apply for the extra days as Unpaid leave. Hajj, maternity and paternity leave are arranged with HR using paper forms.

F5Your payslips

  1. Tap Payslip. Your latest payslip appears after the company has paid salaries for that month.
  2. If there are several, choose the month in Choose pay period.
  3. Tap اردو to read it in Urdu (tap English to switch back).
  4. Tap Download PDF to save it (the PDF is in English).
