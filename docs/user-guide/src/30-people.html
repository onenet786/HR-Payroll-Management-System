PART C
People
Adding and looking after employee records, their documents and their career changes, plus recruitment, performance reviews and company assets. For HR staff.

C1Employee records — 3. Employee Directory

Before you start

Branches, departments, designations and wage types must already exist (sections B2 and B3). The employee form can only choose from those lists.

Add a new employee

  1. Click Onboard Employee (top right). The ONBOARD NEW EMPLOYEE WORKSPACE opens. It has four sections; scroll down through all of them.
  2. Fill Personal & Core Details (see below).
  3. Fill Organization & Regional Assignment: Assigned Branch, then Department, then Designation (each list depends on the one before).
  4. Fill Wage & Custom Allowance Configuration: wage type, salary, bank, account number and IBAN.
  5. Fill Statutory & Trust Compliance: EOBI, FBR tax, social security, provident fund and gratuity.
  6. Click Complete Onboarding. The new employee appears in the list as Active, with today as the joining date.
Warning — the form starts with sample values

Some boxes already contain example data (CNIC 42101-1234567-3, mobile +923001234567, an HBL account, IBAN, EOBI and social-security numbers and a salary of 85,000). Replace every one with the real employee's details. Wrong bank details mean the salary goes to the wrong account.

Wage and bank details

  • Wage Type — Monthly salaried or Daily wager.
  • Basic Monthly Wage / Daily Rate (PKR) — the gross monthly salary (or daily rate). The green box shows how it is split: Basic 50%, House Rent 30%, Conveyance 10%, Medical 10%, with estimated tax and take-home pay. Click Auto-fill FBR Allowances to fill the allowance boxes, or type your own amounts in Allowance Overrides.
  • Tick Take Home (FBR Auto) if you know the agreed take-home amount instead; the system works out the gross for you.
  • Disbursal Bank, Account Number and PKR IBAN Number are required. Click + to add a bank that is not listed.
Important — always check the IBAN

When you pick a bank and type the account number, the system builds a suggested IBAN. It may not be correct. Copy the real 24-character IBAN (it starts with PK) from the employee's bank letter or cheque book.

Statutory & Trust Compliance

  • Enable EOBI — type the EOBI number. If the employee is exempt, untick it and type the reason.
  • Enable FBR Tax — deduct income tax. Type the employee's NTN if they have one, and their provincial social-security number (for example the PESSI number). If they are not registered, type the reason.
  • Opt In PF Fund and Opt In Gratuity — tick if the employee is in your provident fund or gratuity scheme.

Employee codes

Codes look like DTE-PRD-00001:

  • DTE — the first three initials of your company's name (for example "OneNet Solutions" → ONS, "Demo Textiles" → DTE, "Bin Ishaq" → BIS). Words like (Pvt), Ltd, Limited and Pakistan are ignored.
  • PRD — the short code of the department.
  • 00001 — a running number. Numbers are never reused.

If you type a code yourself it must start with your company prefix and must not already be in use. Existing codes are never changed automatically.

Change an employee's details

  1. Find the person in the list (or press Ctrl+K and type their name).
  2. Click Edit Record, change what you need and click Save Changes.
Good to know

Salary increases, promotions and transfers should not be typed here — use 9. Salary Revisions and 12. Employee Lifecycle, so a second person approves them and arrears are calculated. The list has no search box of its own; use Ctrl+K. There is no bulk import from Excel; employees are added one by one.

Employee documents

  1. Click Documents on the employee's row.
  2. Choose the document type: CNIC, Contract, Appointment letter, Certificate, Resume or Other.
  3. Click Upload document and choose the file (PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 10 MB).
  4. Use the download symbol to open a document, or the bin to delete it (you are asked to confirm).

When an employee leaves (Trigger Exit)

  1. Click Trigger Exit on the employee's row.
  2. Choose the Separation type (Resignation, Termination, Retirement, Death or Redundancy) and the last working date.
  3. Type the reason, notice-pay days and leave days to be encashed (filled in from the earned leave balance).
  4. Click Finalize Exit & Disbursal.
Important — this sends the exit for approval

The employee stays Active until another person approves the request in 12. Employee Lifecycle. Approval marks them as left and creates their final settlement in 11. Gratuity & Settlement. The amounts on this screen are an estimate; the settlement uses your gratuity rules exactly. Before paying, collect company assets (section C5).

C2Career changes — 12. Employee Lifecycle

Probation, transfers, promotions, resignations and terminations all go through this page so that a second person approves them before the employee record changes.

Raise a request

  1. On the Requests tab, choose the Employee and Request type.
  2. Set the Effective date and write the Reason / justification (at least 10 characters).
  3. Fill the extra boxes for the request type:
    Request typeExtra information
    Probation ConfirmationNone — approval marks probation as confirmed.
    Probation ExtensionNew probation review date.
    TransferDestination branch, department and designation.
    PromotionNew designation and, optionally, the proposed new salary.
    Resignation / TerminationNotice-pay days and leave days to encash (click "use it" to copy the earned leave balance).
  4. Click Submit for approval.

Approve or reject (a different person)

  1. Requests appear under Awaiting approval with who raised them.
  2. Click Approve (type a short comment) or Reject (type the reason).
When approvedWhat happens
Probation extension / confirmationThe probation date or status on the employee record is updated.
TransferBranch, department and designation change.
PromotionDesignation changes. A higher salary becomes a draft salary revision that must be approved separately in 9. Salary Revisions.
Resignation / TerminationThe employee is marked as left, and a final settlement (gratuity, leave encashment, notice pay) is created in 11. Gratuity & Settlement for payroll to process.

Onboarding checklist

The Onboarding tab lists active employees whose joining paperwork is not complete. Tick by hand: CNIC copy on file, signed appointment letter, bank account verified, HR policies acknowledged. Items marked (auto) tick themselves when the employee record is complete: EOBI registered, social security registered, NTN captured, fingerprint or face enrolled.

Employment letters and experience certificates

On the Letters tab, click Employment letter (current staff) or Experience certificate (staff who have left). A ready-to-print letter opens in a new browser tab; print it with Ctrl+P, sign and stamp it. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for this site in your browser.

C3Recruitment

Post a job

  1. Click Post Job.
  2. Fill Job Title, department, branch, job type, number of vacancies, salary range, experience and qualification, Posted Date, Closing Date and the description.
  3. Click Post Job. (Nothing happens if the title or closing date is empty.)

On the Job Postings tab, use Hold or Filled to change a job's status, and View Pipeline to see its applicants.

Track applicants

  1. Click Add Applicant, choose the job, type the name, email, phone and other details, and save.
  2. On Applicant Pipeline, move each applicant with the → and ← buttons through: Applied, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, Interviewed, Offer Extended, Hired or Rejected.
  3. Click an applicant card to see all details or jump to any stage.
Good to know

Moving an applicant to Hired does not create the employee record. Add the person yourself with Onboard Employee (section C1).

C4Performance reviews

  1. Open Performance and click New Review.
  2. Choose the employee, review period (for example H2-2026) and review date.
  3. For each KPI (Technical Delivery, Quality of Work, Team Collaboration, Communication, Attendance & Punctuality — you can add or remove KPIs), give the weight and the self and manager star ratings. The weights should add up to 100%.
  4. Write strengths, areas for improvement and manager comments. Tick Recommend Salary Increment (with %), promotion, training or improvement plan if needed.
  5. Click Submit Review. It shows Pending HR Approval.
  6. HR clicks Approve Outcomes or Reject Outcomes. An approved increment becomes a draft salary revision; pay changes only after that draft is approved in 9. Salary Revisions.

Employees can see their own approved reviews.

C5Company assets

  1. Add: click Add Asset, type the asset tag (for example ASSET-LPT-004), name, serial number, cost, category, condition and purchase date, and click Add Asset.
  2. Give to an employee: click Assign on an available asset, choose the employee and click Assign.
  3. Take back: click Return and then Confirm Return. The asset becomes Available.
  4. Repairs: click Repair while it is away and Fixed when it is back.
Important — before a final settlement

Check this page for anything still assigned to a leaving employee and collect it before the settlement is paid. The settlement does not check assets automatically.
