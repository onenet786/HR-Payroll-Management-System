PART G
Attendance kiosks
Setting up the Windows attendance kiosk at your entrance, using it every day, and using an Android tablet or phone as a kiosk. For administrators and the staff who look after the kiosk.

G1Installing the Windows kiosk

You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC with a webcam and/or a USB fingerprint reader (DigitalPersona U.are.U 4500 or SecuGen Hamster Pro), and an internet connection.

  1. Run the installer Bin Ishaq Attendance Kiosk Setup 1.0.x.exe given by your IT person. Choose the install folder and finish. Desktop and Start-menu shortcuts are created.
  2. The kiosk starts by itself every time Windows starts. Only one copy runs at a time.
  3. To update to a newer version, just run the newer installer. It closes the running kiosk automatically; punches waiting to be sent are kept. (Version numbers go up with every release — 1.0.1, 1.0.2…; the current version is shown in Settings.)
Tip — first update from version 0.0.0

If updating from the very first version (0.0.0) fails, uninstall "Bin Ishaq Attendance Kiosk 0.0.0" from Windows Settings → Apps, then run the new installer. Your kiosk settings and waiting punches are kept.

G3First start: register the kiosk and choose its branch

  1. An administrator creates a login with role Kiosk Terminal (section B5).
  2. On the kiosk, click the gear (Settings).
  3. Under SERVER REGISTRATION, type the kiosk account's email (or username) and password, and click Register this kiosk. The password is used once and not stored; the PC receives its own secret key.
  4. The line turns green: Registered as "…" on … with …, and the kiosk downloads employees.
  5. Under TERMINAL CONFIGURATION, set the TERMINAL ID (for example KIOSK-WIN-01), LOCATION / DESCRIPTION (for example Main Gate) and — required — the ASSIGNED BRANCH. The kiosk keeps only that branch's employees.
  6. Click Save Settings.

Warning — Clear Local Cache

The Danger Zone button Clear Local Cache permanently deletes punches stored on this PC that have not been sent yet. Use it only when your IT person asks, and after a successful Sync.

G4Punching in and out at the kiosk

With a fingerprint

  1. On the Fingerprint tab, (optionally) type your code.
  2. Place the enrolled finger flat on the reader (or press SCAN FINGERPRINT).
  3. The result card shows your name and Punch In Recorded or Punch Out Recorded.

With your face — several people at once (Multi-Face Stream)

  1. On the Camera tab choose Multi-Face Stream (Boxes).
  2. Walk up and look at the camera for a moment. Each face gets a box; when recognised it shows your name and PUNCHED IN · time or PUNCHED OUT · time.

With your face — one at a time (1-on-1 Challenge)

  1. Choose 1-on-1 Challenge (Oval). Only one person should be in front of the camera.
  2. Put your face inside the oval and hold still (or press CAPTURE & PUNCH).
  3. Follow the prompts: Center your face → Turn LEFT → Return to center → Turn RIGHT → Live person verified. A photo is saved as evidence.

Staff from another branch

Use the Camera tab, type your complete employee ID first, then complete the face check. This needs internet; fingerprint cannot be used for another branch.

G5Internet problems, syncing and exiting

  • Every punch is saved on the PC first and then sent to the server. If the internet is down, punches wait (Pending Sync above 0, Synced light amber) and are sent automatically every 5 minutes, or when you press Sync. The server accepts waiting punches up to 8 days old — fix internet problems quickly.
  • New employees, faces and fingerprints reach the kiosk at the next sync.
  • If the sync line says Not registered — open Settings to register this kiosk, register it again (section G3). This also happens if an administrator clicked Disconnect.
  • To close the kiosk, click Exit and confirm Exit Kiosk. Attendance cannot be recorded until it is started again.

G7Using an Android tablet or phone as a kiosk

  1. Install the mobile app on the tablet (section F1).
  2. Sign in with a Kiosk Terminal account (not an employee's). The screen turns sideways and fills the whole screen in kiosk mode, opening straight on face recognition.
  3. Tap the gear to open Mobile Kiosk Settings: set the Terminal ID, location and assigned branch (or company-wide), and whether to punch automatically when one face is centred. Tap SAVE KIOSK SETTINGS.
  4. Employees stand in front of the camera. Choose Multi-Face Stream (walk-in, several people) or 1-on-1 Liveness (one person, head-turn check). A success screen says ACCESS GRANTED and Welcome … Check-In registered at ….
  5. To leave kiosk mode, open the gear and tap LOG OFF / EXIT KIOSK.

Buttons in the tablet settings: START / RETRY CAMERA, ANDROID CAMERA PERMISSION (opens the app permissions) and FLUSH LOCAL WEB CACHE (does not delete saved attendance).
