A1How to use this guide

This guide explains every screen of the system, one step at a time. You do not need any technical knowledge. Follow the numbered steps in order.

  • Words in a green box like Save Settings are exactly what you will see on screen — a button, a menu item or a field name. Look for those words and click (or tap) them.
  • Text in italics is a message the system shows you.
  • Pictures show the screen you should see. Your company name, people and amounts will be different.
Tip — read only what you need

Find your job in the table "Who does what" (section A3), then read Part A and the parts listed for your job.

Coloured boxes

A2What the system does

The system keeps all your staff information in one safe place and uses it to pay people correctly under Pakistani law. In simple words:

  1. You record your company and your rules once — tax slabs, EOBI, provincial social security (PESSI / SESSI etc.), minimum wage, leave days, holidays.
  2. You add your employees — personal details, CNIC, job, shift, salary and bank account.
  3. Employees mark attendance — at a kiosk with their face or fingerprint, or on their phone with face check and GPS when working outside the office.
  4. HR checks attendance and leave and approves anything unusual.
  5. Each month the attendance register is locked, so nobody can change it after payroll uses it.
  6. Payroll is calculated (tax, EOBI, social security, provident fund, loans), checked by a second person, and a bank file is produced for salary transfer.
  7. Employees see their payslips in the mobile app (in English or Urdu), and you produce the FBR, EOBI and social-security files.
Punch→Attendance register→Lock month→Payroll run→Second-person approval→Bank file & payment→Payslips & returns

Two-person checking ("maker-checker")

Many important actions need two different people: one person prepares the change (the maker) and another person checks and approves it (the checker). The system will not let you approve your own work — the approve button is greyed out for you. This protects the company and you from mistakes and fraud.

A4First-time setup of a brand-new system

This happens only once, the very first time the system is opened and no accounts exist yet. If your company already uses the system, skip this section.

  1. Open the web address of your system. Because there are no accounts yet, the DATABASE SETUP WIZARD appears instead of the sign-in page.
  2. Fill in Company Name, Super Admin Full Name, Super Admin Email and Super Admin Password. The boxes contain sample text — replace all of it.
  3. Choose a strong password of at least 12 characters. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe.
  4. Click Initialize Database & Sign In. You are signed in as the first Super Admin.
  5. Now work through the numbered menu items in order, starting with 1. Statutory Config (Part B), and set up two-step verification (section A6).
Good to know

Once any account exists, the setup wizard can never be used again — this stops anyone from taking over your system. If the wizard says This system is already set up. Sign in with an existing account., use the normal sign-in page.

A5Signing in and signing out

  1. In EMAIL, type your email address.
  2. In PASSWORD, type your password. Click the eye symbol to see what you typed. If you see Caps Lock is ON, press the Caps Lock key once.
  3. Tick Remember me for 5 days only on your own computer or phone (never on a shared one).
  4. Click Sign In to System.
  5. If your account uses two-step verification, the TWO-FACTOR VERIFICATION box appears:
    • Authenticator app: open the app on your phone (for example Google or Microsoft Authenticator), read the 6-digit code and type it, then click Verify Authenticator Code.
    • Email code: click Send email code, open your email, type the 6-digit code and click Verify Email Code. Wait one minute before asking for another code.

How long you stay signed in

  • Normally you are signed out after 30 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours in any case.
  • With Remember me for 5 days ticked, you stay signed in for up to 5 days on that device. After you enter a verification code once, that device will not ask for a code again for 5 days — even after you sign out.
  • When your time runs out you see Your secure session expired. Please sign in again. Just sign in again; nothing is lost.

Forgot your password?

For security, passwords are not reset by email. Contact your HR or system administrator. After confirming who you are, they set a new password for you in User Management → Edit → Reset password (see section B5). Then sign in with the new password.

Signing out

Click Logout at the top right of the screen. Always sign out on shared computers.

A6Protecting your account (two-step verification / MFA)

Two-step verification means signing in needs your password and a 6-digit code from your phone or email. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot get in. Everyone who can see salaries or change settings must set it up — the box My Security at the top of every page shows ACTION NEEDED until you do.

Set up with an authenticator app (recommended)

  1. Install Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone (free, from the Play Store).
  2. In the portal, click Set up MFA and then Authenticator app. A square QR code appears under Connect your authenticator app.
  3. In the phone app, tap + / Scan a QR code and point the camera at the code. (If you cannot scan, type the Secret shown under the code into the app instead.)
  4. Type the 6-digit code the phone shows into Authenticator code and click Confirm MFA. Do this within 10 minutes.
  5. You see Authenticator MFA is enabled. The badge changes to Protected.

Set up with email codes

  1. Click Set up MFA → Email verification. A 6-digit code is emailed to you.
  2. Type it into Email verification code and click Confirm email MFA.
Important — never share your codes

No one from HR or IT will ever ask for your 6-digit code. Do not tell it to anyone.

Lost or changed your phone?

  • If you can still sign in (for example on a remembered device): in My Security click Lost or replacing your authenticator device? Request a controlled MFA reset., explain how your identity can be checked, and click Submit recovery request.
  • If you cannot sign in at all: contact your administrator. They raise the reset request for you (section B5).
  • Two different administrators are needed: one raises the request, another approves it. After approval, you sign in with your password only — then set up MFA again straight away.
  • If a phone was lost or a device was shared, click Stop trusting my devices (lost phone or shared device) in My Security. Every device will ask for a code again at the next sign-in.
