Search for anything (Ctrl + K)

  1. Press Ctrl + K on the keyboard, or click the search box at the top.
  2. Type part of a page name ("payroll", "holiday") or a person's name, employee code or CNIC.
  3. Click a result. Press Esc to close the search.

Choosing an employee opens the Employee Directory; find the person in the list there. The pages "User Guide & Labor Laws" and "Notification Center" are also easiest to open from this search.

Urdu screens (اردو)

The menu, page help and common buttons change to Urdu, and payslips show English and Urdu side by side. Names, amounts and codes stay as they are. The official record is always the English version. Your choice is remembered on this computer.

Notifications

  • Click the bell (or search "Notification") to open the Notification Center. Click an item or Mark read when you have dealt with it.
  • HR and payroll staff can click Send Notification to send a message to one employee or to everyone (leave Target empty). You may add an Urdu title and message. Employees see it in their mobile app.
Good to know

"Mark All Read" and deleting a notification currently only change your screen; after refreshing the page the items may return.

Typing dates

All dates are shown as DD-MMM-YYYY, for example 05-Nov-2026. In any date box you can type 05-Nov-2026, 5 Nov 2026, 05/11/2026 or 05-11-2026 (day first), then press Enter — or click the small calendar symbol and pick a day. A date box turns red if the date is not valid.

Messages and buttons that are greyed out

  • If a button is grey, move the mouse over it: a short note explains why (for example Prepared by you; another authorized user must approve.).
  • If you see Access Denied, your role does not include that page. Ask your administrator.
  • Nothing is ever deleted without a confirmation, and every change is recorded with your name and the time.

A8The Operations Dashboard — your daily starting point

The dashboard opens after you sign in. It shows what needs attention today. Everything on it comes from the same records that payroll uses, so if it looks right here, it is right in payroll.

Live payroll readiness

Shown to administrators and payroll staff. It lists the checks that must pass before you pay salaries from this system alone. Each check has a green tick (done), a red mark (action needed) or a dotted circle (optional), and tells you which page fixes it.

Three separate payroll usersCreate logins for preparer, approver and payer (User Management).
Multi-factor authentication on privileged accountsEvery admin and payroll user sets up MFA (section A6).

Compliance alerts

A list of problems found in the last 45 days. Each alert shows a severity — CRITICAL (fix now), WARNING (fix soon) or INFO — a category and the employee. Use the category list to filter, tick Show info to include minor items, click CSV to download the list, or Fix records to open the Employee Directory.

Summary cards and approval inbox

  • Active Headcount — active employees out of all employees.
  • Daily Attendance — percentage of staff who punched in today.
  • Pending Tasks — leave requests, attendance corrections and payroll items waiting, and how many are overdue.
  • Payroll Disbursals — an estimate of the monthly basic salary bill.
  • Multi-Branch Locations — each branch with its city, province and staff count.
  • Manager Approval Inbox — the five oldest items waiting for a decision. Items waiting two days or more are red. Click Review to open the right page.
Tip — a 2-minute daily routine for managers
  1. Clear red items in the Manager Approval Inbox.
  2. Look at Compliance alerts, starting with CRITICAL.
  3. Check Daily Attendance for missing staff.
  4. Look for red badges in the menu and the step bar.
