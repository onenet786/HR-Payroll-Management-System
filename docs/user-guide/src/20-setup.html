PART B
One-time setup
For administrators. Do these steps once, in this order, before adding employees or running payroll. Afterwards you only come back when a law, a rate or your company changes.
  • 1. Statutory Config — legal rates and company rules (B1)
  • 2. Company & Master Data — your company (B2)
  • Master Data — branches, departments, designations, wage types (B3)
  • 4. Holiday Calendar — public holidays (B4)
  • User Management — logins, roles and kiosks (B5)

B1Legal rates and company rules — 1. Statutory Config

This page holds everything payroll needs from the law and from your company policy: income-tax slabs, EOBI, provincial social security, minimum wage, leave days, gratuity rules, alert limits, bank file layouts and accounting codes.

How changes work here (very important)

  • There is no Save button. Every number you type is saved immediately as a proposal.
  • Payroll keeps using the old (live) rates until a different authorised person approves your proposal.
  • While a proposal is waiting, the page shows the proposed values, and the box at the top shows Pending proposal by … since … — changed: ….
  • Only one proposal can be open at a time.

To change a rate (the person making the change)

  1. Open 1. Statutory Config.
  2. In Source of these rates, type the official notification number or web link (for example "Finance Act 2026, FBR withholding card TY2027"). This is required before approval.
  3. Click Attach notification (PDF/image) and choose the official document (PDF, JPEG or PNG).
  4. Change the values you need (the sections are explained below).
  5. Ask a colleague with the Settings permission to approve.

To approve or reject (the second person)

  1. Open 1. Statutory Config. The amber box shows the waiting proposal and what changed.
  2. Compare the values with the official notice.
  3. Click Approve & apply and type a short comment (at least 5 characters), for example the notice number you checked. The new rates now apply to payroll.
  4. Or click Reject and give a reason. (If you made the proposal yourself, your button says Withdraw instead.)

Income-tax slabs

Each slab row has: Min Annual (PKR), Max Annual (PKR), the fixed Base Tax and the Rate.

Important — type the rate as a percentage

Type 11 for 11%, 1 for 1%, 35 for 35%. (Older screens suggested typing 0.11; the system now reads such small numbers correctly as well, but whole percentages are clearer.)

  • Download CSV saves the table for your records.
  • Update from FBR does not go online. It replaces your slabs with the table built into the program, which may be from an earlier year. Always compare with the current Finance Act before approving.
  • A 10% surcharge is added automatically when yearly taxable income is above PKR 10 million (or the threshold you set).
Important — every July

The tax year changes on 1 July. Before the first July payroll, update the tax year, effective date and slabs, and have them approved. Otherwise payroll refuses to run with Payroll refused: statutory configuration is not valid for this period.

Bank, EOBI and social-security file templates

  1. Choose a template in the list. ✓ means verified with the bank; ⚠ means not yet verified. Templates exist for HBL, UBL, Meezan, MCB, Bank Alfalah, Allied and Standard Chartered, plus EOBI and social-security schedules.
  2. Click Copy to start from an existing one, or New for an empty one.
  3. Using your bank's official file specification, fill Name, Purpose, Layout (Delimited or Fixed Width), Delimiter, Debit (payroll funding) account and Specification reference.
  4. Edit the columns (title, value, format, required). Use + Add column to add one.
  5. Look at Preview (one sample employee). Fix any red errors.
  6. Send a small test file to your bank. Only after the bank accepts it, tick Verified with a bank-accepted test batch.
Good to know

The "Generic bank advice CSV" is for internal checking only — it is not a bank upload format. Any change to a template removes its "verified" tick, and template changes need approval like rates.

Watch panels further down the page

Read-only lists that warn you early: CNIC Compliance Watch (CNICs expiring within 90 days), Contract & Probation Watch, Identity & Payment Collision Watch (two employees with the same CNIC or bank account — this blocks payroll), Attendance Compliance Alerts, Payroll Release Control and Today's Attendance Exceptions.

B2Your company — 2. Company & Master Data

A five-step form that creates (or updates) your company, its main branch, starting departments and designations, and the basic payroll settings.

  1. Company identity: Legal name, Display / trading name, Company code, Industry, NTN (all required), plus legal type, fiscal year start (July), payroll frequency, currency and time zone. Click Continue.
  2. Office & branch: the registered office address, city and province. The main branch is "Head Office" at the same address unless you untick Same as registered office.
  3. Organization: at least one department (name and short code, for example "Production" / PRD) and, optionally, designations with their department and grade. Use Add row.
  4. Payroll compliance: tax year, effective date, minimum wage, EOBI employee and employer %, social security %, wage ceiling, gratuity days, provident-fund maximum, overtime multiplier, monthly hours, and whether you are registered with EOBI and social security (enter the registration numbers).
  5. Review: check the summary, tick I reviewed these details and statutory settings. and click Create primary company (or Update primary company).
Important
  • The payroll values entered in step 4 become live straight away. Later changes should be made in 1. Statutory Config, where they need approval.
  • The short department codes you type (PRD, HR, FIN) become part of every employee code, so keep them short.

B3Branches, departments, designations and wage types — Master Data

Use Master Data (under SYSTEM in the menu) to add or change lists after the first setup. The tabs are Branches, Departments, Designations, Zones, UC/Towns and Wage Types.

  1. Choose a tab and click Add … (for example Add Branch), or Edit next to an existing record.
  2. Fill the form and click Save record.
TabWhat to enter
BranchName, code, city, province (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan or Islamabad/ICT), address, geofence (latitude, longitude and radius in metres — the area in which phone punches count as "on site"; 50–200 m is recommended) and establishment type (Factory / Shop / Industrial / Other).
DepartmentBranch, department name, code.
DesignationDepartment, title, code, grade.
Zone, UC/TownOptional geographic groupings used for reports.
Wage TypeName, code and calculation basis: Monthly (salaried) or Daily (daily wagers). Every employee needs one.
Tip — finding a branch's latitude and longitude

Open Google Maps, right-click the middle of the branch building and click the two numbers shown at the top (for example 31.4504, 73.1350). The first is latitude, the second longitude.

Good to know

Codes are created automatically (tick off Auto-generate code to type your own: capital letters, numbers and single hyphens). Records cannot be deleted, and a record already used by employees cannot be moved to another branch or department.

B4Public holidays — 4. Holiday Calendar

Holidays here are paid days off and are not counted as absences.

  1. Open 4. Holiday Calendar and click Add Holiday.
  2. Type the HOLIDAY NAME and the DATE, and choose the TYPE: Public, Company or Optional.
  3. Tick Recurring annually only for holidays on the same date every year (14 August, 23 March, 25 December…).
  4. Click Add Holiday.

To change or remove a holiday, move the mouse over it and click the pencil or the bin. Use the year list and the All / Public / Company / Optional buttons to filter.

Important — Eid and other moon-based holidays

Do not mark Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Ashura or 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal as recurring. Add them each year once the official dates are announced.

Good to know — shifts, Ramadan and Fridays

There is no separate shift page. Each employee's shift times, grace minutes, Ramadan timings and Friday timings are set on the employee's own record (section C1).
