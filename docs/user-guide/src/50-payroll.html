E2Loans and salary advances — 8. Loans & Advances

  1. Click Apply for Loan/Advance.
  2. Choose the Employee and the Type (Salary Advance or Loan), type the Amount (PKR), and move the slider to choose the number of monthly instalments (1–24).
  3. Type the Reason and click Submit Application. It is saved as Pending.
  4. To approve, click Approve & Disburse (or Reject). The loan becomes Active.
  • Each month the instalment is deducted in payroll. The balance goes down only when that month's payroll is marked Disbursed. When nothing remains, the loan becomes Closed.
  • The cards show Repaid, Remaining instalments and a progress bar. Filter with the employee list and the Pending / Active / Closed / Rejected buttons.
Important

Loans do not require a second approver in the system. Follow your company's own approval process (for example a signed loan form) before clicking Approve & Disburse. If Submit does nothing, choose the employee again in the list and resubmit.

E3Salary increases — 9. Salary Revisions

  1. Click Process Increment.
  2. Choose the Employee and Revision Type (Annual Increment, Promotion, Adjustment or Market Correction).
  3. Type the New Salary (PKR) and the Effective Date, and the reason.
  4. Click Submit Revision for Approval. It shows DRAFT — NOT APPLIED; pay has not changed yet.
  5. A different person clicks Approve & Apply. The salary now changes.
Good to know — back-dated increases

If the effective date is in a month already paid, the system works out the difference and creates an Arrears item for the current month. It appears in 10. Payroll Processing → One-time payroll adjustments and must be approved there before the next payroll.

E4Running payroll — 10. Payroll Processing

Payroll runs move through these stages:

Draft→ approved by a second person →Approved→ bank file, then paid →Disbursed

A Draft or Approved run can be Cancelled (with a reason). A Disbursed run can never be cancelled or deleted — corrections go into a later month.

Step 1 — Lock the attendance register

  1. At the top right, choose the month and year.
  2. Click Lock Register. You see Attendance register locked. Integrity reference: …
If you seeDo this

…because it contains no attendance records.You chose the wrong month, or no punches were recorded.

Need to correct a locked month? Click Reopen Register (vN), type the reason (at least 10 characters), make the corrections, then click Lock Corrected Register. A month cannot be reopened while a payroll run uses it (cancel the run first), and never after it has been paid.

Step 2 — One-time items (bonus, arrears, deductions)

  1. In ONE-TIME PAYROLL ADJUSTMENTS, choose the employee and type: Bonus, Commission, Arrears, Reimbursement, Deduction or Tax Adjustment.
  2. Type the amount and reason. For bonus, commission and arrears, tick Include in taxable salary if it is taxable.
  3. Click Submit for approval. Another payroll user clicks Approve or Reject.

Each item is paid by one payroll run only. Items approved after the month's run are paid with a supplementary run (below).

Step 3 — Create the payroll run

  1. Click Run New Payroll Cycle. The system first checks everything; if a check fails you get a clear message (see the table below).
  2. When it succeeds you see Draft payroll created for N employees… with total gross and net pay. Only Active and On-Leave employees are included.
  3. Check the Payroll Preview table: gross, FBR tax, EOBI, social security, provident fund and net pay per person. Read any material pay variance warning (pay changed by 25% or more since last month).
  4. Click View Slip to open any employee's payslip.

Reading a payslip

  • Earnings components: basic pay, house rent, medical, conveyance, overtime and any bonus or arrears.
  • Statutory deductions: FBR income tax (with tax year, taxable income so far, tax already deducted and projected yearly income), EOBI, provident fund, loan recovery, absence and other deductions.
  • Net Take-home pay at the bottom.
  • Buttons: English + اردو (adds Urdu labels), PDF (downloads the payslip), Print / Save as PDF, Close.

Step 4 — Approval by a second person

  1. A different payroll user signs in, opens the month and checks the draft and a few payslips.
  2. They click Approve on the run. (For the person who prepared it, the button is grey: Prepared by you; another payroll officer must approve.)

Step 5 — Bank file

  1. On the approved run, click Bank Advice File.
  2. Choose the Bank file template (✓ verified, ⚠ not yet verified) and the Value date.
  3. Check the beneficiary list (name, IBAN, bank, amount) and the total.
  4. Click Download bank file, then upload it in your bank's corporate portal and authorise it there.
Warning

The bank file is not a payment by itself — money moves only when you upload and authorise it in your bank's portal. Use only a template your bank has accepted. If any IBAN is invalid or an amount is zero, the file is blocked until you fix the employee record.

Step 6 — Mark as paid

  1. When the bank has paid, click Mark Disbursed and type the bank's transaction / reference number.
  2. The run becomes Disbursed. Each employee gets a "Salary has been disbursed" notification, loan balances are reduced, and payslips appear in the mobile app.

The person who approved the run cannot mark it paid, and the bank file must be downloaded first.

Yearly and statutory reports

In Statutory, finance & off-cycle payroll, set the Tax year and download: PF ledger, Gratuity provision, Tax deposit reconciliation, Annual tax summary, Missing registrations and Tax vs annual liability. To give an employee their yearly salary tax certificate (section 149), choose them and click Open certificate, then print. Only approved and paid runs are counted.

Recording the FBR tax deposit (CPR)

  1. After paying the withheld tax to FBR, find the run under FBR deposits pending reconciliation and click Record CPR.
  2. Type the CPR / PSID number, the amount deposited and the deposit date (YYYY-MM-DD).

Corrections after a month is paid

Paid months stay locked. Use Retroactive attendance correction: choose the paid payslip, type + or − days and the reason, and click Raise; or fix the employee record and click Recalculate month & raise difference. This creates an arrears or deduction item in the current month for another payroll user to approve.

Bonuses after the month's run — supplementary payroll

Click Run supplementary payroll for M/YYYY. It pays one-time items approved after the regular run, taxed at the employee's top rate. Then approve, download the bank file and mark it paid as usual.

Final settlement payroll

Settlements processed in 11. Gratuity & Settlement appear under Approved settlements awaiting payroll. Click Create final settlement run to pay one, with the year's tax recalculated and outstanding loans recovered. Then approve, bank file and mark paid as usual.

When the bank returns a payment

  1. On the paid run, click Payment exceptions.
  2. Click Mark failed next to the employee and type the bank's reason.
  3. Correct the employee's bank details in 3. Employee Directory.
  4. Tick the failed rows and click Download retry advice; upload it to the bank.
  5. After the bank pays, click Record retry paid and type the bank reference.

Cancelling a run

On a Draft or Approved run click Cancel Run and type the reason (at least 10 characters). Payslips are kept for audit; one-time items are released for the next run, and the register can be reopened. If the run had been approved, a Reversal journal CSV is offered for your accounts.

Checking against your old payroll (parallel run)

Before relying on the system alone, compare one month with your previous payroll: in Parallel run against your current payroll choose an approved run, upload your old payroll for the same month as a CSV file (employee code or CNIC, plus gross, tax, EOBI, PF, loan, deductions and net), review the Matched / Differences counts, download the Difference report, explain any differences and click Record result on this run.

E5Final settlements — 11. Gratuity & Settlement

Settlements are created automatically when a resignation or termination is approved in 12. Employee Lifecycle. You can also create one here:

  1. Click New Settlement.
  2. Choose the employee, SEPARATION TYPE and LAST WORKING DATE. Joining date, basic salary and years of service are shown.
  3. Type ENCASHABLE LEAVE DAYS, NOTICE PAY DAYS (OWED) and PENDING SALARY DAYS.
  4. Check the Settlement Breakdown and total, then click Save Settlement. It is Pending.
  5. A second payroll user clicks Process.
  6. Pay it either with a final settlement payroll run (section E4) or, after paying outside payroll, a third person clicks Mark Paid.

Gratuity follows your rules in 1. Statutory Config (minimum service, part-years, resignation percentage). Employees with less than the minimum service get no gratuity. Collect company assets before paying (section C5).
