# Browser Video Walkthrough Script

**Target duration:** 12–15 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording with English voiceover and familiar Pakistan payroll terms

| Scene | On-screen action | Voiceover | Why/decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening | Show title and platform badges | “Welcome to Bin Ishaq HR & Payroll Management System, a Pakistan-focused HRMS across web, Windows, Android and kiosk.” | Establish product scope |
| 2. Login | Enter a demo account and sign in | “Every session begins with a role-linked account. The role decides which HR and payroll functions are visible.” | User decides which authorized identity to use |
| 3. Dashboard | Review summary cards | “The dashboard is the operational starting point. It tells management where attention is needed before opening individual registers.” | Decide which exception/module to investigate |
| 4. Employees | Open directory and add form | “The employee master holds organization, compensation, bank and Pakistan statutory identity data used by connected modules.” | Decide correct branch, wage and statutory setup |
| 5. Attendance | Change date/view and inspect records | “Attendance captures presence, late arrival, half-day, absence and overtime—the evidence payroll consumes.” | Decide whether a record is valid or needs regularization |
| 6. Mobile punch | In walkthrough, simulate camera/GPS card | “Employees can punch with supported biometric or camera flows while GPS is recorded where permission is available.” | Decide punch method and confirm location/time |
| 7. Leave | Submit then show approval | “Approved leave distinguishes paid entitlement from unpaid deduction. Cross-month leave is counted only inside the payroll period.” | HR decides approve or reject |
| 8. Payroll preview | Choose month/year and inspect register | “The preview combines employees, attendance, approved leave, loans and effective statutory settings.” | Payroll specialist checks exceptions before creating Draft |
| 9. Lifecycle | Create Draft, approve, mark disbursed | “The controlled sequence protects history. Loans post only at approval, and employee-level results become immutable snapshots.” | Authorized user decides readiness at each gate |
| 10. Payslip | Open payslip | “The payslip explains earnings, deductions, employer contributions and net take-home.” | Verify employee result |
| 11. Bank advice | Open and download CSV | “Bank advice comes from the saved payroll snapshot. Its layout must still be confirmed with the organization’s bank.” | Decide whether file matches bank specification |
| 12. Statutory settings | Show tax slabs and province rates | “FBR slabs, effective dates, EOBI basis and provincial social-security values are configurable because rules change.” | Admin decides whether verified legal updates are required |
| 13. Supporting HR | Cycle through loans, revisions, performance, assets, recruitment, gratuity | “These modules preserve the decisions surrounding payroll and the employee lifecycle.” | Managers record approvals, custody and outcomes |
| 14. Access control | Show roles/users | “Least-privilege roles protect payroll, biometric and personal information.” | Admin decides permission scope |
| 15. Kiosk | Show kiosk simulation | “The dedicated kiosk records attendance without exposing administrative navigation.” | Employee identifies and punches |
| 16. Close | Show logic map and checklist | “The system’s value comes from connected evidence: employee setup, attendance and leave become controlled payroll and auditable output.” | Confirm next operational task |

## Recording notes

- Record at 1920×1080 with browser zoom at 90–100%.
- Use demo data only; do not expose real CNIC, bank, password or biometric information.
- Pause 1–2 seconds after every navigation.
- Add captions for Draft, Approved, Disbursed and “Verify with bank.”
- Use the interactive walkthrough as the visual storyboard.
